# Kite's photographs

Replaced on 16 October 2026: the Figma file's own photographs were taken down
(some were not decent for a shop front, and some showed brands and famous
faces that are not ours to use). In their place, black leather shoots from
Unsplash, each checked on its own page to be under the **Unsplash License**
(free for commercial use, no credit required; Unsplash+ photos excluded).

Each sits at the **same address** the old one had (`/kite/<hash>.png`), so
every page, every saved shop setting and the Theme Store demo shows the new
photograph with nothing else changed. The hash names are now just names; they
no longer match the files' contents. Each is cropped to its slot's own shape
(faces kept in frame), as a PNG like the originals.
